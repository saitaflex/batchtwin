# BatchTwin — 4-Minute Pitch Script

**Spoken English · ~580 words · ≈ 4:00** at pitch pace (130–145 wpm)  
**Slides:** advance with the timing marks below (see `Final_Presentation_Slides.md`)  
**Techniques:** Simon Sinek (start with *why*) · Steve Jobs (contrast + rule of three + pause) · Nancy Duarte (*what is / what could be*) · Guy Kawasaki (problem → magic → business) · TED (concrete story, one human moment)

---

## Delivery cheat sheet

| Technique | How you use it here |
|---|---|
| **Sinek — Why first** | Open on *why factories deserve truth*, not on software features |
| **Jobs — Contrast** | “Today: paper. Tomorrow: a living record.” Pause after the turn |
| **Jobs — Rule of three** | Cost. Quality. Integrity. Then: true cost · SPC · Part 11 |
| **Duarte — What is / could be** | Alternate pain → relief twice before the product name |
| **Kawasaki — Magic slide** | One demo beat mid-pitch; don’t narrate every feature |
| **TED — Specificity** | One real product: 800 bottles of magnesium syrup |
| **Churchill — Anaphora** | Repeat “Not a scan. Not a form. A twin.” |
| **Obama — Inclusive we** | “We built…” then hand the future to the audience |

**Stage notes:** stand still for the opening line · gesture on the three pains · walk one step on “BatchTwin” · point to screen only on demo · silence 1 full second before the closing line.

---

## Script (with timing + slide cues)

### [0:00 – 0:35] OPENING — Why · Slide 1 → 2  
*(Sinek + Jobs cold open)*

Imagine eight hundred bottles of magnesium syrup — already filled, already labeled — sitting in quarantine.

Not because the formula failed.  
Not because the machine broke.  
Because the **paper** wasn’t ready.

*[pause]*

That paper is the batch record — the legal dossier that decides whether a lot can be sold. In factories like Laboratoires Medicka in Nabeul — GMP since 2010, three hundred products — manufacturing already runs on **Odoo**. But the dossier that matters… still walks the plant in four Word documents. Handwritten. Signed with a pen. Fragile.

Industry already knows the cost of poor quality: about **fifteen percent of revenue**. And on the floor, that cost looks like this —

### [0:35 – 1:10] PROBLEM — What is · Slide 2  
*(Duarte “what is” + rule of three)*

**Cost** is fiction. Odoo knows the theoretical recipe — never the powder that went to the air, never the drop on the scale.

**Quality** is late. Every thirty minutes an operator measures ten bottles… and writes the numbers in a binder. The drift that could have warned them at three p.m. is discovered after the shift is over — after the scrap is made.

**Integrity** is a hope. Paper can be lost. Back-dated. Filled in afterwards. Nothing breaks when someone lies to the page.

*[beat]*  
So the factory has a modern ERP… and a nineteenth-century truth.

### [1:10 – 1:45] TURN — What could be · Slide 3  
*(Jobs contrast + memorable line)*

What if the batch record wasn’t a document you file at the end —  
but the thing you **work in** — and it computed as you filled it?

*[advance slide]*

That is **BatchTwin** — the living batch record.

Odoo says what *should* happen.  
BatchTwin captures what *actually* happens — every gram, every check, every signature — and produces a compliant, costed batch record automatically.

Not a scan of paper.  
Not another form in the cloud.  
**A twin of the batch.**

Pharma-grade traceability. Real cost. Predictive quality. Carbon per unit.  
At SME price — **without ripping out Odoo.**

### [1:45 – 2:35] MAGIC — Demo story · Slide 4  
*(Kawasaki “underlying magic” + TED specificity — narrate as if demoing)*

Here’s the magic — and you can watch it live in three minutes.

An operator signs in. Identity first — always.  
They weigh. Instantly the tiles show **true cost** versus theoretical — and the euros that never reach Odoo.

On Quality, fill checks become a live control chart. Push the line with a little drift… and BatchTwin doesn’t wait for scrap. It forecasts the breach — *adjust the filler now* — and if a check fails, a **deviation opens by itself**. Nobody has to remember to raise it.

Sign Production: the password is demanded again. That’s Part 11 — not a dropdown of names.  
Try Release early? Refused. The system names exactly what’s blocking.

Close the deviation with a root cause and a CAPA — signed again.  
One click: the PDF binder. Same records. Plus kWh and CO₂ per bottle.

Falsify a row in the database? The integrity badge goes red. Recompute the whole chain to hide it — and the **external anchor** still catches it.

*[half-smile]*  
That’s not a demo trick. That’s how trust is built.

### [2:35 – 3:15] WHY US / WHY NOW — Market · Slides 5–7  
*(Kawasaki competition in one breath)*

Who pays? SME supplement and small-batch pharma plants already on Odoo — the ones enterprise MES will never prioritize. Six-figure tools. Six-to-eighteen-month rollouts. Not built for Odoo.

Paper is cheap and painful. Odoo Quality alone is solid ERP — but not a true GMP batch record.  
**We are the missing layer** between paper and enterprise MES.

We don’t replace the ERP. We complete it.  
Pilot with Medicka. Grow through Odoo integrators. Licence per site. Optional VÉRA for raw-material intelligence. Setup, validation, support.

Estimated impact on a typical line: on the order of **thirty skilled hours a week**, material losses finally measured — tens of thousands of euros a year — release in hours, not days.

### [3:15 – 3:40] TEAM & TRACTION — Slides 8–10  
*(credibility without boasting)*

We are Oussama Labidi, Salem Amara, and Ahmed Hammami.  
Our design partner is real: Medicka. Our prototype runs: full lifecycle, e-signatures, hash-chained audit, digitized Medicka dossiers, SPC, PDF, fifty-three tests green. EN, FR, and Arabic. Tablet-ready.

Next: live Odoo. One line. IQ, OQ. Then scale.

### [3:40 – 4:00] CLOSE — Ask · Conclusion slide  
*(Jobs encore + clear CTA — slow down)*

*[step forward · silence 1 second]*

Every bottle that leaves a plant carries a story.  
Today that story is trapped in a binder.  
Tomorrow it can be a digital twin — self-documenting, self-costing, self-auditing, carbon-aware.

**Replace the binder. Keep Odoo.**

Give us a three-minute demo — or one line at Medicka.  
And let’s make the batch record finally tell the truth.

*[stop. smile. hold for applause / questions]*
