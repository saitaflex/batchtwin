What's inside

Everything the paper binder did — and everything it couldn't

Paperless batch record

The four dossiers — fabrication, conditionnement, qualité, libération — with role-gated electronic signatures and one-click release.

Live mass balance

Real consumption incl. losses vs. the theoretical BOM → the true cost per unit, turning invisible waste into a tracked KPI.

Predictive SPC

The 10-bottle fill check becomes a live control chart that forecasts drift — “underfill in ~22 min” — before scrap is made.

Tamper-evident audit

Every action hash-chained and e-signed — ALCOA+ / 21 CFR Part 11. Alter one record and the chain visibly breaks.

Energy & CO₂ per batch

IoT sensors meter each station; energy is split across the four dossiers → kWh and CO₂ per unit — a product carbon passport.

Local AI copilot

Ask “why is this batch blocked?” and get grounded answers — 100% local (Ollama), nothing leaves the plant.
